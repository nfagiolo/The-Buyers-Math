Understanding Your Roof Replacement Estimate

A roof replacement is one of the highest-stakes exterior renovations for any home. In 2026, roofing costs continue to be dictated by petroleum asphalt indices, regional skilled labor availability, and updated residential building codes (IRC Section R905).

5 Contractor Red Flags to Watch For

  • No Mention of Ice & Water Shield: In climate zones with winter freeze cycles, building codes mandate self-adhering polymer-modified bitumen underlayment extending at least 24 inches inside the interior wall line.
  • Vague Plywood Decking Rates: Shady contractors quote low upfront bids and then charge $120+ per 4x8 plywood sheet for rotted decking replacement discovered after tear-off. Standard fair rates are $70 to $95 per sheet.
  • Reusing Old Drip Edge or Flashing: Quality contractors always install new corrosion-resistant step flashing and kick-out flashing along chimneys and side walls.
  • Demanding 50%+ Upfront Deposit: Reputable roofing companies typically ask for 10% to 30% upon material delivery, with the final payment due only after full site magnetic nail sweep and municipal inspection.
  • Refusing to Provide Proof of Workers' Compensation: Roofing carries high risk. If an un-insured worker is injured on your property, homeowner liability policies can be challenged.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical roof replacement take?

For an average 1,500 to 2,500 sq ft single-family home, a 5-to-6 person crew completes full tear-off and reshingling in 1 to 2 days, weather permitting.

Can I install a new roof over existing shingles?

While IRC code permits up to 2 layers, an overlay prevents inspection of rotted roof decking, voids most manufacturer wind warranties, and traps heat that degrades new shingles prematurely.
