Understanding Your Roof Replacement Estimate

A roof replacement is one of the most critical home investments you can make. In 2026, roofing costs continue to be driven by material supply chains, regional labor rates, and building code requirements for underlayment and ice-and-water barriers.

Key Factors That Influence Your Quote

  • Roof Size vs. Home Footprint: Due to pitch slope and overhangs, actual roof surface area is typically 25% to 40% greater than your home's ground footprint.
  • Pitch and Complexity: Steeper roofs require staging and harness equipment, increasing labor hours by 15% to 30%.
  • Decking Inspection: Water-damaged or soft plywood sheathing must be replaced before underlayment is applied.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical roof replacement take?

For an average 1,500 to 2,500 sq ft single-family home, a professional roofing crew usually completes tear-off and new shingle installation in 1 to 2 days, weather permitting.

Can I install new shingles over existing shingles?

While some municipal building codes permit up to two layers of asphalt shingles, roofing experts strongly recommend a complete tear-off to inspect decking for rot and ensure proper manufacturer warranty coverage.
