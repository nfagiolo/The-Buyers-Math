Understanding Your Roof Replacement Quote

Replacing a roof is one of the most critical maintenance tasks for any homeowner. The cost varies widely based on geographic location, the materials chosen, and the specific geometry of your home.

What is a "Roofing Square"?

Contractors often price jobs by the "square." In roofing terminology, one square equals 100 square feet. If your roof is 2,000 square feet, your contractor will order materials and quote labor for 20 squares.

Key Factors Impacting Price

  • Material Lifespan: Asphalt shingles are the most affordable upfront but typically last 15-25 years. Metal roofing costs nearly double, but can last 50+ years and lower your homeowner's insurance premiums.
  • Tear-Off Costs: If your current roof has two or more layers of old shingles, building codes require them to be stripped to the deck, increasing labor and dumpster disposal fees.
  • Deck Repair: If your contractor finds rotted plywood decking (sheathing) under the old shingles, replacing those boards will be an additional cost added to your final bill.
