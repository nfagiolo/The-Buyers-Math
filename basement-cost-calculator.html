Planning Your Basement Remodel

Finishing a basement is one of the most cost-effective ways to add livable square footage to your home. Because the foundation, roof, and exterior walls are already in place, the cost per square foot is generally much lower than an outward home addition.

Understanding the Costs

  • The Basics: Framing, running electrical wiring, insulating the walls, and hanging drywall are the core components of turning an unfinished space into a habitable room.
  • Adding Plumbing: If your basement isn't already roughed-in for plumbing, trenching the concrete floor to add a bathroom, wet bar, or kitchenette will significantly increase your baseline costs.
  • Egress Requirements: Building codes require emergency egress windows for basement bedrooms. If your basement doesn't currently have one, cutting the foundation and installing a window well will add $3,000 to $5,000 to your budget.
