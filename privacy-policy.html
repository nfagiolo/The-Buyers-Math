Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At The Buyer's Math (accessible at thebuyersmath.com), the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy document details the types of information collected and recorded by The Buyer's Math and how that information is utilized.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in privacy by design. We do not require account registration or login credentials to access our 21 interactive cost calculators, daily market commodity briefings, or buying guides.

  • Client-Side Calculator Inputs: All project cost parameters (such as roof square footage, heat pump tonnage, panel amperage, or material selections) are computed locally in your web browser. We do not store or transmit individual calculation inputs to external servers.
  • Log Files & Standard Telemetry: Like virtually all websites, The Buyer's Math utilizes standard log files. Information logged includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), referring/exit pages, date/time stamps, and click patterns. This information is used strictly to analyze aggregate trends, maintain site security, and optimize performance.
  • Contact Inquiries: If you reach out to us directly via our contact form or email, we receive your email address and message contents solely for responding to your inquiry.

2. Cookies, Web Beacons & Third-Party Services

The Buyer's Math uses cookies to store information about visitors' preferences, optimize website speed, and ensure proper functionality across sessions.

3. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

Google is an advertising partner on our website. Google uses cookies, including DART cookies, to serve advertisements to site visitors based on their visit to thebuyersmath.com and other websites across the internet.

  • Visitors may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ad & Content Network Privacy Policy.
  • You can customize or disable personalized ads at any time via Google Ads Settings.
  • Additional consumer opt-out tools are available through the Network Advertising Initiative at www.aboutads.info.

4. Affiliate Advertising Disclosure

The Buyer's Math participates in verified affiliate advertising programs designed to support our free independent research and data modeling:

  • Amazon Associates Program: We are a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases made through outbound product links (tagged with tag=nfagiolo-20).
  • CJ Affiliate (Commission Junction): We partner with Commission Junction (Publisher PID: 101896838) to connect homeowners with verified, licensed local contractors through partner networks such as Angi. Tracking cookies are placed solely to credit lead generation referrals.

5. Google Analytics (GA4)

We use Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID: G-V4GTQSCFW6) to collect aggregated, anonymized metrics on page traffic and reader engagement. Google Analytics operates using client-side cookies without collecting personally identifiable information.

6. CCPA & CPRA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have the right to request disclosure of personal data collected, request deletion of personal information, and confirm that a business does not sell personal information. The Buyer's Math does not sell or rent personal data under any circumstances.

7. GDPR Compliance (General Data Protection Regulation)

For visitors from the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the right to access, update, or request erasure of any personal communication data we hold. Inquiries can be submitted directly through our contact page and will be addressed within 30 days.

8. Contact Information

If you have any questions or require additional details regarding our privacy practices, please contact our editorial staff at contact@thebuyersmath.com or visit our Contact Us page.
