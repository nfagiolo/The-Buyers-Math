The 2026 Guide to Window Replacement Costs

Replacing the windows in your home is one of the highest-ROI projects you can undertake, both for energy efficiency and resale value. In 2026, the national average cost to replace a standard window ranges from $450 to $1,200, with most homeowners spending around $800 fully installed.

Vinyl vs. Wood vs. Fiberglass

Vinyl windows remain the most popular and budget-friendly choice. They require practically zero maintenance and offer excellent insulation, typically costing between $400 and $800 installed. Fiberglass is rapidly gaining market share due to its structural stability and ability to mimic wood without the rot risk, though it commands a premium at $500 to $1,200 per window. Wood frames offer premium, historic aesthetics but require the most upkeep and typically start around $1,000 per window.

Do You Need Triple-Pane Glass?

Double-pane glass filled with argon gas is the standard baseline today. Upgrading to triple-pane glass adds about 15% to 20% to the cost of the window. If you live in an extreme climate with harsh winters, or if your home is near a busy highway and requires soundproofing, triple-pane is a worthwhile investment. Otherwise, a high-quality double-pane window with a Low-E coating is highly efficient for most regions.
