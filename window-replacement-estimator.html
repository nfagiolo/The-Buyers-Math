Pocket Retrofit vs. Full Frame Tear-Out

  • Insert / Pocket Replacement: New windows slip directly into existing wood jambs without disturbing exterior siding or interior drywall casing. Highly economical ($500–$900 per opening).
  • Full-Frame Replacement: Entire window, frame, and sill are removed down to rough framing. Required if existing jambs suffer rotted wood or water intrusion ($900–$1,600+ per opening).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is double-pane glass sufficient or should I upgrade to triple-pane?

Double-pane low-E argon-filled windows deliver optimal cost-performance for most climate zones. Triple-pane glass is recommended for extreme northern climates (Zone 6+) or homes adjacent to high-noise highways.

Are replacement windows eligible for federal tax credits?

Yes. Energy Star Most Efficient certified replacement windows qualify for a 30% federal tax credit under Section 25C up to $600 annually.
