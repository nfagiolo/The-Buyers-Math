Standby Generator Sizing & Installation Guide (2026)

A permanently installed home standby generator monitors utility voltage 24/7. When a grid blackout occurs, an automatic transfer switch (ATS) safely isolates your home from utility lines and engages the generator within seconds.

Essential Circuit vs. Whole-House Sizing

  • Essential Circuit (10–14 kW): Powers selected critical loads via an emergency subpanel (refrigerator, furnace blower, sump pump, WiFi, and select room lighting circuits).
  • Whole-House (18–26 kW): Powers the entire 200-amp main electrical panel without power rationing, running central air conditioners, electric ranges, and water heaters simultaneously.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a standby generator run continuously for several days?

Yes. When connected to a municipal natural gas line, a standby generator can run indefinitely during extended grid outages, requiring only periodic oil checks after 100 hours of continuous operation.

How far must a generator sit from windows and doors?

National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) standards typically mandate a minimum 5-foot clearance from operable windows, exterior doors, and soffit vents to prevent carbon monoxide intrusion.
