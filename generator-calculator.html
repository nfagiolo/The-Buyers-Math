Running Watts vs. Starting Watts

Generators are rated by two numbers. Running watts are the continuous power needed to keep your appliances running. Starting watts (surge) are the extra burst of power required for a few seconds to start electric motors, like those in refrigerators and well pumps.

Your generator must be large enough to handle the total running watts of all connected items, plus the highest starting surge of the largest single motor you plan to run.

Connect Safely During Outages

Never plug a generator directly into a wall outlet. Ensure you have the proper heavy-duty extension cords to safely route power to your essential appliances.
