How Much Does It Cost to Run a Pool Pump?

Your pool pump is likely the second largest consumer of electricity in your home, right behind your HVAC system. A standard single-speed pump running 8 to 12 hours a day can easily add $50 to $100 to your monthly utility bill.

Single-Speed vs. Variable-Speed Pumps

If you are still using a single-speed pump, upgrading to a variable-speed (VS) pump is one of the highest ROI home improvements you can make. VS pumps run at lower speeds for circulation and filtering, consuming up to 80% less electricity. Most homeowners recoup the cost of a variable-speed pump in energy savings within the first two years.
