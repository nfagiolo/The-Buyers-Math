Tank vs. Tankless Water Heaters

The average traditional tank water heater lasts 8 to 12 years before the internal tank begins to rust and leak. Replacing like-for-like is the cheapest immediate option.

Upgrading to a tankless system provides endless hot water and saves space, but the installation cost is significantly higher. Plumbers often have to upgrade gas lines to accommodate the high BTU demand of a tankless unit, and specialized venting must be installed through the wall or roof.
