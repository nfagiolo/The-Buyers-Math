Understanding Your Ductless Mini-Split Quote

Ductless heat pumps deliver extraordinary 20+ SEER2 efficiency and independent room-by-room temperature control. Because they eliminate leaky central sheet metal ducts, they cut heating and cooling energy waste by up to 30%. However, proper installation requires EPA-certified refrigerant flaring and deep vacuum micron pulls.

4 Mini-Split Contractor Red Flags to Watch For

  • Making Copper Flares with Cheap Plumbing Flaring Bars: Mini-split systems run at high operating pressures (exceeding 400 PSI on R-410A / R-32). Technicians must use an eccentric cone orbital flaring tool and a digital torque wrench. Hand-tightened flares leak refrigerant within 1 to 2 years.
  • Skipping the 500-Micron Triple Evacuation Vacuum Test: Air and moisture inside copper lines combine with synthetic POE oil to create hydrofluoric acid, destroying the compressor. Reputable technicians pull a vacuum below 500 microns and perform a 30-minute standing decay test.
  • Failing to Install a Snow Stand / Bracket in Cold Climates: Condensers installed directly on ground patio slabs get buried in winter snowdrifts and freeze into ice during defrost cycles. Condensers must be elevated on wall brackets or 12-to-18-inch snow stands.
  • Leaving Bare Refrigerant Lines Exposed to UV Sunlight: Technicians must enclose all outdoor copper lines and electrical cables in rigid UV-resistant line hide ducting (such as Fortress or Slimduct) to prevent weather cracking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a mini-split heat an entire house in winter?

Yes, provided you install cold-climate hyper-heat equipment rated down to -15°F. Sizing each zone based on a Manual J heating load calculation ensures continuous warmth without auxiliary baseboard heat.

How often do ductless mini-splits need filter cleaning?

Indoor air handler mesh filters should be rinsed in warm water every 4 to 6 weeks. A professional deep coil cleaning and blower wheel antimicrobial wash is recommended once every 1 to 2 years.
