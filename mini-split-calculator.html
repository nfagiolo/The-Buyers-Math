Why Choose a Ductless Mini-Split?

Ductless mini-splits are incredibly efficient heating and cooling systems. They require no ductwork, making them perfect for older homes, new additions, or specific rooms that are always too hot or too cold.

Understanding Installation Costs

The total cost is largely driven by the number of indoor units (heads) you need connected to the outdoor condenser. High-efficiency "hyper-heat" models designed for cold climates cost more upfront but qualify for significant federal tax credits and state rebates.
