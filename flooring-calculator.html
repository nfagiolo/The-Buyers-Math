Understanding Flooring Material Specs, Subfloor Flatness & Labor Quotes

Over 80% of premature Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP) locking-joint failures and engineered hardwood hollow spots trace back to skipped subfloor preparation rather than defective planks. NWFA (National Wood Flooring Association) and rigid-core SPC manufacturers mandate that concrete slabs and OSB subfloors be leveled to within 3/16 of an inch over a 10-foot radius (or 1/8 inch over 6 feet). When comparing contractor quotes, always verify whether self-leveling portland cement compound, high-spot diamond grinding, and 6-mil polyethylene vapor barriers are included in the base square-foot rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What wear-layer thickness should I look for on Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP)?

Avoid builder-grade 6-mil or 12-mil vinyl planks in main living areas; specify a minimum 20-mil commercial urethane wear layer with ceramic bead finish and a minimum 5.5mm–6.5mm total plank thickness so locking tongues do not shear under furniture loads.
