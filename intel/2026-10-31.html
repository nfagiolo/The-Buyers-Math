5. Code & Tax Incentive Brief: IRC R310 - Emergency Escape and Rescue Openings (Egress Windows) + Alternative Fuel Vehicle Refueling Property Credit - 26 U.S.C. § 30C Compliance & Rebates

Basement Bedroom Egress Window Code Dimensions: Under International Residential Code (IRC) Section R310, all basements with habitable space and every sleeping room must have at least one emergency escape and rescue opening. The opening must have a minimum net clear opening of 5.7 sq ft (5.0 sq ft at grade floor), minimum clear height of 24 inches, minimum clear width of 20 inches, and max sill height of 44 inches above floor.

  • ✓ Clear opening measured with sash fully open (height x width >= 5.7 sq ft).
  • ✓ Window well projection of at least 36 inches with permanent ladder if depth exceeds 44 inches.
  • ✓ Gravel base and connection to perimeter footing drain or sump basin.
