Understanding Your Residential Fence Quote

A privacy fence defines property boundaries, secures pets and children, and adds immediate privacy. However, fence longevity depends almost entirely on below-grade structural details: post depth, concrete footing volume, and fastener corrosion resistance.

4 Fence Contractor Red Flags to Watch For

  • Setting Posts Shallow Above the Frost Line: In freeze-thaw climates, setting posts only 18 to 24 inches deep guarantees frost heaving that causes fences to lean after one winter. Posts should extend 30 to 36 inches deep (below local frost lines) with 4 to 6 inches of crushed gravel base for water drainage.
  • Using Electro-Galvanized Nails on Pressure-Treated Wood: Pressure-treated chemicals (ACQ / copper azole) rapidly corrode cheap electro-galvanized fasteners, causing dark chemical streaks and detached pickets within two years. Contracts must mandate hot-dipped galvanized or 304/316 stainless steel ring-shank nails or exterior screws.
  • Skipping 811 Utility Call Before You Dig: Handymen who dig without requesting free 811 underground utility locates risk puncturing gas lines or cutting fiber-optic cables, leading to thousands in municipal fines and repair costs.
  • Failing to Check Property Pin Surveys: Building a fence even a few inches over a legal boundary line can result in disputes with neighbors and court-ordered demolition. Always verify iron property boundary pins or obtain a boundary survey prior to augering holes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far apart should fence posts be spaced?

Standard residential privacy fencing requires posts spaced exactly 6 or 8 feet on center. Spacing posts wider than 8 feet causes horizontal 2x4 rails to sag under the weight of wet wood pickets or strong wind loads.

Does a new fence require a building permit?

Most municipalities require a zoning or building permit for fences over 6 feet tall or fences placed in front yard setback zones. Fencing around swimming pools strictly requires self-closing, self-latching gates meeting local pool barrier safety codes.
