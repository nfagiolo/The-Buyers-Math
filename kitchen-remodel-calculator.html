Understanding Your Kitchen Remodeling Quote

A kitchen remodel delivers the highest lifestyle enhancement and one of the highest resale recovery percentages of any interior renovation. However, contractor bids often vary wildly due to cabinet construction grades, countertop fabrication surcharges, and hidden trade mechanical scope.

5 Kitchen Remodeling Red Flags to Watch For

  • Demanding 50%+ Upfront Deposit: Reputable general contractors operate on milestone payment schedules (e.g. 10% upon signing, 30% upon cabinet delivery, 30% upon drywall/cabinet rough-in, 20% on counter installation, and 10% final retainage after punch list signoff).
  • Vague Cabinet Specifications: Bids that list "custom wood cabinets" without specifying plywood boxes vs. particle board (MDF), soft-close dovetail drawers, or full-extension undermount glides often cut corners on quality.
  • Missing Dedicated Electrical Circuit Scope: Modern 2026 NEC codes mandate multiple dedicated 20A branch circuits for countertop small appliances, plus dedicated circuits for the dishwasher, garbage disposal, microwave, and refrigerator.
  • Excluding Countertop Fabrication & Sink Cutouts: Unscrupulous contractors quote material square footage pricing and then bill $400 to $700 extra as change orders for sink cutouts, cooktop cutouts, and polished edge profiles.
  • No Builder's Risk or Workmanship Warranty: Ensure your contractor provides an explicit 1- to 2-year written workmanship warranty covering grout cracking, cabinet door alignment, and plumbing leaks.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a full kitchen remodel typically take?

A minor cosmetic update takes 2 to 3 weeks. A complete midrange remodel typically spans 6 to 9 weeks, with the longest lead time being cabinet delivery (4–8 weeks) and countertop templating to install (7–12 business days).

Should I refinish/paint existing cabinets or buy new?

If your cabinet boxes are solid 3/4-inch plywood and your current layout functions well, professional spray painting or refacing saves $8,000 to $18,000 compared to full box replacement.
