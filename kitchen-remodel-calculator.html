📋 Contractor Vetting Checklist: 4 Questions to Ask Before Signing

Protect your home and budget by verifying these critical technical standards with prospective contractors:

✓ Confirm Detailed Milestone Payments

Never pay more than 10-15% upfront deposit. Tie subsequent payments to completed inspection milestones.

✓ Establish a 15% Contingency Buffer

Concealed electrical wiring or outdated plumbing behind walls commonly adds 10-15% in unforeseen change orders.

✓ Specify Cabinet Construction Specs

Confirm solid plywood boxes (not particle board) and full-extension soft-close dovetail drawer boxes in the contract.

✓ Inspect Dust Containment & Site Protection

Require negative air pressure HEPA scrubbing and floor plastic barriers to protect living areas from drywall dust.

Kitchen Remodeling Cost Guide (2026)

Kitchen remodels consistently deliver strong homeowner satisfaction and retain 60% to 75% of their value upon resale. Understanding the breakdown of your project expenditure helps avoid unexpected change orders.

NKBA Rule of Thumb

The National Kitchen & Bath Association suggests budgeting 10% to 15% of your home's total market value for a comprehensive kitchen renovation to avoid over-improving or under-building for your neighborhood.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most expensive part of a kitchen remodel?

Cabinetry and hardware make up the single largest cost category, typically claiming 28% to 35% of the total budget.

How much can I save by keeping the same layout?

Keeping your sink, dishwasher, and stove in their original locations avoids relocating gas lines, drain vents, and 240V circuits, saving $3,000 to $6,000 in trade rough-in labor.
