The 2026 Guide to Kitchen Remodeling Budgets

The kitchen is the heart of the home, and remodeling it is one of the most popular and impactful renovations you can undertake. However, it is also one of the most complex. This calculator breaks down typical expenses to help you align your dream design with your actual budget.

Key Variables in This Calculator

  • Project Scale: A cosmetic refresh (painting cabinets, new hardware, updating appliances) is highly affordable. A "gut renovation" that strips the room down to the studs is a major investment.
  • Cabinetry: Cabinets generally consume the largest portion of a kitchen budget. Stock or ready-to-assemble (RTA) cabinets are economical, semi-custom offers flexibility, and fully custom built-in cabinetry represents a premium luxury expense.
  • Countertops: Laminate is budget-friendly, while natural stone (granite, marble, quartzite) and engineered quartz command higher prices per square foot but offer superior durability and aesthetic appeal.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is the typical return on investment (ROI) for a kitchen remodel? Depending on your local market, a minor kitchen remodel yields an average ROI of 70% to 80% at resale. Over-improving for your neighborhood can lower that return.
  • Where can I save money? Keeping the existing layout (not moving plumbing or gas) and opting for high-quality RTA cabinets or refinishing existing boxes can save tens of thousands of dollars.
