Understanding Your Bathroom Remodeling Quote

A well-executed bathroom renovation consistently recovers 60% to 72% of its cost on resale. However, because bathrooms concentrate plumbing stacks, dedicated electrical circuits, and waterproof tile within tight square footage, labor costs frequently exceed material purchases.

4 Bathroom Contractor Red Flags to Watch For

  • No Continuous Shower Waterproofing Membrane: Tiling directly over regular drywall or standard greenboard without a certified bonded membrane (such as Schluter-KERDI, Hydro Ban, or RedGard) is the leading cause of structural wall rot.
  • Skipping the 24-Hour Shower Pan Flood Test: Reputable contractors plug the shower drain and fill the pan with 2 inches of standing water for 24 hours to prove zero leakage into lower floors before laying tile.
  • Vague Demolition Scope for Subfloor Rot: Water damage around toilets and tubs is common. Contracts should state an hourly or square foot rate for subfloor sistering rather than an open-ended surprise change order.
  • Venting the Exhaust Fan into the Attic: Building codes strictly require bathroom exhaust fans to vent through the exterior roof or sidewall to prevent severe attic mold growth.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a standard 5x8 bathroom remodel take?

A standard pull-and-replace takes 2 to 3 weeks. A complete gut with plumbing relocation spans 3 to 5 weeks due to mandatory inspection rough-ins and tile curing times.

Why is a 15% contingency buffer essential for bathrooms?

Demolishing existing tubs frequently exposes hidden subfloor rot, corroded galvanized pipes, or ungrounded wiring that must be remedied before new fixtures are installed.
