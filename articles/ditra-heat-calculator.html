Schluter DITRA-HEAT Installation Cost Guide

Installing electric floor warming in your home provides luxurious comfort and efficient heating. Understanding the total installation cost for a Schluter DITRA-HEAT system requires factoring in materials, room size, and electrical or tile labor.

Key Components and System Products

To set up an electric underfloor heating system, you will need several key products:

  • Schluter DITRA-HEAT Membrane: Uncoupling membrane designed to secure heating cables and protect tiles from cracking.
  • Schluter DITRA-HEAT Cable: Flexible heating cable embedded into the membrane mats to supply evenly distributed heat.
  • Schluter DITRA-HEAT Thermostat: Programmable or Wi-Fi digital thermostat to monitor and control floor temperatures.

Cost Breakdown

Average costs typically range between $10 and $25 per square foot for materials and professional installation:

  • Membrane Mats: Approximately $2.00 to $3.50 per sq. ft.
  • Heating Cables: Approximately $5.00 to $8.00 per sq. ft.
  • Thermostat: $150 to $300 depending on smart/Wi-Fi options.
  • Labor & Installation: $5.00 to $12.00 per sq. ft. for tiling and electrical wiring.
