In 2026, the total installed cost for a digital thermostatic shower system ranges from $2,450 to $6,800, while a DIY material kit cost ranges from $850 to $3,150 depending on the outlet configuration (2-port vs. 4-port). Choosing between the Kohler Anthem and the Moen U by Moen (marketed alongside the Moen Smart Shower series) requires analyzing more than just screen aesthetics. The mechanical rough-in architecture, electrical supply demands, and drywall/tile modification costs dictate your final budget. While Moen utilizes a consolidated electronic mixing valve that simplifies basic retrofits, Kohler’s Anthem system relies on high-flow digital thermostatic processor blocks that excel in multi-outlet luxury builds with body sprays and steam integrations.

Licensed Trade Labor Breakdown: Plumbing, Electrical, and Tile

Installing a digital shower valve is a multi-trade project. Unlike a mechanical pressure-balance valve that mounts directly inside the wet wall behind the handle, digital mixing valves are installed in an adjacent dry cavity—such as a closet, vanity cabinet, or basement ceiling—within 20 to 30 feet of the shower stall.

Licensed Plumbing Labor ($115 – $185 per hour)

A licensed plumber requires 6 to 12 hours depending on wall access and pipe material (PEX-A expansion vs. Type L copper sweat connections). Total plumbing labor averages $750 to $2,100.

  • 2-Port Systems (Kohler K-28209-NA / Moen S3102): Plumbers run 1/2-inch supply lines into the valve and two 1/2-inch outlet lines to the fixtures (e.g., rain head and handshower). Labor takes roughly 6 to 8 hours in open-stud conditions.
  • 4-Port Systems (Kohler K-28211-NA / Moen S3104): The Kohler K-28211-NA demands 3/4-inch dedicated hot and cold trunk lines from the water heater to prevent pressure drops when running four outlets simultaneously. Upgrading existing 1/2-inch copper to 3/4-inch lines adds $450 to $900 in plumbing labor and materials.

Licensed Electrical Labor ($95 – $150 per hour)

Both the Kohler Anthem and Moen Smart Shower valves require a dedicated power supply. An electrician will charge $285 to $600 (3 to 4 hours) to run 14/2 Romex from the nearest circuit or subpanel and install a code-compliant receptacle inside the valve access cavity. If you opt for Kohler’s Anthem+ system with integrated digital lighting and steam generators (e.g., Kohler Invigoration series), electrical labor increases by $800 to $1,500 to run a dedicated 240V, 40-amp to 60-amp circuit.

Tile & Drywall Modification ($15 – $35 per sq. ft. / $350 – $900 lump sum)

In a retrofit where existing tile must remain intact, a tile setter must diamond-core a precise 1.5-inch to 2.25-inch hole for the digital controller mounting plate and data cable, plus patch the old 6-inch mechanical valve hole using a remodel cover plate or replacement tiles. Expect to pay a $350 to $600 minimum trip charge for precision porcelain or natural stone coring.

Code Compliance, Permits, and 2026 Federal Tax Credits

Because digital valves conceal electronic actuators and pressurized water connections inside wall cavities, building inspectors strictly enforce accessibility and electrical safety codes.

  • 2026 NEC (National Electrical Code) Rules: Under NEC Article 406.9(C) and 210.8(A), the 120V AC outlet powering the digital mixing valve must be GFCI-protected. Furthermore, neither the receptacle nor the digital mixing valve can be placed inside the wet zone, and the outlet must remain accessible without removing permanent building finishes.
  • IRC (International Residential Code) Plumbing Rules: IRC P2708.3 mandates that all shower valves meet ASSE 1016 or ASME A112.18.1 anti-scald thermostatic standards. Both Kohler Anthem and Moen S3102/S3104 valves feature built-in electronic thermostatic protection that caps default max temperatures at 120°F. However, code strictly requires a permanent, removable service access panel (minimum 12"x12" for Moen, 14"x18" for Kohler 4-port) at the valve location to service the internal solenoid cartridges and inlet screens.
  • Municipal Permitting: A combined residential plumbing and electrical alteration permit costs $125 to $350 in most U.S. jurisdictions.
  • 2026 Federal Tax Credits (25C / 25D / 30C): Under the Inflation Reduction Act guidelines for 2026, smart digital shower valves and EPA WaterSense-labeled showerheads do not qualify for the Section 25C Energy Efficient Home Improvement Credit, Section 25D Residential Clean Energy Credit, or Section 30C EV Charger Credit. Section 25C plumbing credits are strictly restricted to high-efficiency water heating equipment (such as ENERGY STAR heat pump water heaters, which qualify for up to $2,000). However, check with your municipal water authority; many local utilities in drought-prone states (CA, NV, AZ, CO) offer $50 to $150 local rebates when pairing a digital flow-monitoring valve with 1.75 GPM WaterSense trim.

Exact Installation Preparation & How to Save 20%–30% on Hardware

General contractors and plumbing subcontractors routinely mark up specialty digital plumbing hardware by 25% to 40% over wholesale supply-house pricing to cover warranty liability and procurement time. Because a Kohler Anthem or Moen 4-port system requires multiple components (valve, controller, mounting brackets, and 4 separate fixture trims), contractor markup easily adds $600 to $1,200 in pure overhead to your invoice.

How to Capture the 20%–30% Direct-Buy Savings

You can eliminate this markup by purchasing the exact rough-in and trim SKUs directly from authorized online plumbing distributors or manufacturer storefronts, then hiring a plumber on a strict time-and-materials (T&M) basis. To prevent costly jobsite delays where a plumber charges $150/hour while waiting on missing parts, follow this exact pre-installation preparation checklist before the trades arrive:

  • 1. Map the Dry-Wall Service Cavity: Identify an adjacent closet wall, linen cabinet, or vanity base within 20 feet of the shower stall. Frame a dedicated recess between standard 16-inch on-center 2x4 studs and purchase a paintable flush-mount access panel (such as a 14"x14" Acudor hinged steel door, ~$45) so the plumber can mount the valve immediately.
  • 2. Pre-Install Isolation & Drain Valves: Buy two lead-free brass full-port ball valves with integrated drain bleeder caps ($18 each) for the hot and cold supply inlets, plus brass hammer arrestors (ASSE 1010 rated, ~$22 each). Digital solenoid valves close instantaneously, which will cause severe water hammer in rigid copper pipes if arrestors are omitted.
  • 3. Run a 3/4-Inch Smurf Tube (ENT Conduit): Before closing up drywall or cement board, run a continuous 3/4-inch flexible non-metallic conduit (Smurf tube) with sweeping 90-degree bends from the digital valve cavity to the shower controller location. If the proprietary 30-foot data cable ever fails years after tile installation, this $15 conduit allows you to pull a replacement cable in 10 minutes without demolishing your shower wall.
  • 4. Verify Water Heater Recovery & Sediment Filtration: Digital thermostatic valves contain micro-mesh inlet screens that clog easily from hard water scale or PEX construction debris. Purchase an inline spin-down sediment filter ($65) for the main trunk line, and ensure the plumber flushes both hot and cold lines for 3 full minutes into a bucket before connecting the Kohler K-28209-NA or Moen S3102 valve body.
