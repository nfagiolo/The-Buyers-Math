Understanding Your Residential Solar Quote

Rooftop photovoltaic solar represents one of the highest-yielding home improvements available, generating 10% to 18% annual internal rates of return (IRR). However, navigating door-to-door sales pitches, inflated financing dealer fees, and evolving utility net metering policies requires rigorous diligence.

4 Solar Contractor Red Flags to Watch For

  • Misrepresenting the 30% Federal Tax Credit as a 'Free Government Grant': The Section 25D credit reduces your federal income tax liability dollar-for-dollar. If you lack sufficient tax liability, you cannot monetize the credit immediately. It is never a direct cash rebate or government check.
  • Opaque Financing Dealer Fees (25% to 35% Markups): Low advertised interest rates (e.g. 3.99% or 4.99%) frequently conceal hidden 'dealer fees' tacked onto the principal loan balance, raising a $24,000 cash project to $34,000+. Always request the cash price breakdown first.
  • Installing Over an Aging Roof (10+ Years Old): Solar panels carry 25-year power production warranties. If your asphalt shingle roof fails in year 8, removing and re-installing panels costs $3,500 to $6,000 out of pocket.
  • Promising 1:1 Net Metering in NEM 3.0 Utility Territories: In net billing jurisdictions, daytime exported solar electricity is credited at wholesale avoided cost (~4¢ to 6¢/kWh) rather than retail rates (~35¢/kWh). A battery storage system is essential to store excess energy for evening usage.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 30% Section 25D federal tax credit work?

Under the Inflation Reduction Act, homeowners who purchase their system outright (cash or solar loan) can claim 30% of total project costs—including panels, inverters, racking, labor, and battery storage—on IRS Form 5695.

Should I buy or lease my solar panel system?

Direct ownership (cash or unsecured credit union solar loan) consistently delivers 2.5x to 3x higher lifetime savings than third-party power purchase agreements (PPAs) or solar leases, which often include 2.9% annual cost escalators and complicate home sales.
