📋 Contractor Vetting Checklist: 4 Questions to Ask Before Signing

Protect your home and budget by verifying these critical technical standards with prospective contractors:

✓ Verify Roof Shingle Lifespan

If your roof is over 12-15 years old, replace shingles first; removing panels later for re-roofing costs $150-$250 per panel.

✓ Confirm Utility Interconnection & Net Metering

Verify in writing whether your local utility operates under 1:1 net metering or avoided-cost export rates (NEM 3.0).

✓ Compare Inverter Architectures

Evaluate microinverters (Enphase) vs string inverters with DC optimizers (SolarEdge) for roof shading resilience.

✓ Review 25-Year Production Warranties

Ensure linear production warranties guarantee at least 85% rated power output at year 25.

Residential Solar Payback Guide (2026)

Installing a rooftop solar panel system allows homeowners to lock in electricity expenses and protect against rising utility rates. The federal 30% residential clean energy tax credit significantly reduces capital outlay.

How the 30% Federal Tax Credit Works

Under Section 25D of the Internal Revenue Code, homeowners who purchase their solar PV system (cash or loan) can deduct 30% of total system equipment and installation costs directly from their federal tax liability.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do solar panels still generate power on cloudy days?

Yes. Photovoltaic panels generate approximately 10% to 25% of their rated capacity on overcast days using diffuse ambient light.

What happens to my solar panels after 25 years?

Tier-1 solar manufacturers guarantee panels will produce at least 80% to 85% of their initial output after 25 years of continuous operation.
