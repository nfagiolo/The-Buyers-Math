Installing solar panels is a powerful way to reduce your carbon footprint and lock in your energy costs. However, the initial capital required can be substantial. This calculator helps you determine how long it will take for your utility savings to pay off your initial investment.

Understanding the Solar Tax Credit

The federal residential solar energy credit is a crucial part of the payback equation. Currently, the Residential Clean Energy Credit allows you to claim 30% of the installation costs on your federal income taxes. This applies to the panels, labor, and even integrated battery storage systems.
