Replacing an aging HVAC system represents one of the single largest capital expenditures a homeowner will encounter. Fortunately, the Energy Efficient Home Improvement Credit (Section 25C) provides substantial federal tax relief for households transitioning to high-efficiency electric heat pumps.

Quick Rule of Thumb (2026 Tax Year)

You can claim 30% of total installation costs up to $2,000 annually for qualified air-source or cold-climate heat pumps. Because Section 25C resets each year, homeowners can pair an HVAC heat pump in year one with a hybrid heat pump water heater in year two for an additional $2,000 credit.

To claim the full $2,000 credit on IRS Form 5695, your installation must satisfy specific federal criteria:

  • Principal Residence: The heat pump must be installed in an existing home located in the United States that you own and use as your primary residence. (New construction does not qualify).
  • CEE Highest Tier Standard: The heat pump must meet or exceed the Consortium for Energy Efficiency (CEE) highest tier specifications (excluding advanced tiers).
  • AHRI Certificate: Your contractor must provide an AHRI (Air-Conditioning, Heating, and Refrigeration Institute) certificate verifying that the matched outdoor unit and indoor coil meet efficiency thresholds.
