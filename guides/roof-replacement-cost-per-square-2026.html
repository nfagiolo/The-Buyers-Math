Roof Replacement Cost per Square in 2026: Complete Homeowner's Pricing Guide

Published on October 3, 2026 | Reviewed by The Buyer's Math Building & Estimating Team

When contractors bid on a residential re-roofing project, they calculate pricing using roofing squares rather than standard floor square footage. One roofing square represents exactly 100 square feet of roof surface. Understanding this unit cost prevents overpaying for tear-offs, pitch premiums, and decking repairs.

Quick Pricing Rule of Thumb (2026 National Averages)

Expect to pay $450 to $650 per square for standard architectural laminated shingles on a moderate-pitch roof (4/12 to 7/12 slope). For a typical 25-square roof (approx. 1,800–2,200 sq ft home footprint), total installed costs range between $11,250 and $16,250.

2. Hidden Line Items on Your Contractor's Estimate

  • Tear-Off and Disposal ($100 – $150 / square): Removing 1 existing layer of shingles and hauling to landfill. A second layer adds $40–$60/square.
  • Plywood Decking Replacement ($75 – $110 / sheet): 4x8 CDX plywood or OSB sheets replacing water-damaged or sagging wood.
  • Ice & Water Shield ($80 – $120 / roll): Self-adhering modified bitumen required along eaves and valleys in northern climate zones.
  • Flashing & Ridge Ventilation ($500 – $1,200 total): Step flashing around chimneys, sidewalls, and continuous ridge vent installations.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a 'square' in roofing estimation?

A roofing square equals 100 square feet of roof surface area. For example, a 2,000 sq ft single-story home with overhangs and roof slope typically has approximately 24 to 28 roofing squares.

How much does a square of architectural shingles cost installed in 2026?

In 2026, architectural asphalt shingles cost between $420 and $620 per square fully installed, including old roof tear-off, synthetic underlayment, ice and water shield, flashing, and professional labor.

Is it worth upgrading from 3-tab to architectural shingles?

Yes. The cost difference is typically only $40 to $70 per square, but architectural shingles offer a 30-to-50 year lifespan, 130 mph wind ratings, and significantly higher resale appraisal value compared to 3-tab shingles.
